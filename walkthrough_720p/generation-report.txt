Sixteen clips were generated (all 20 positions are in Selected clips). Four returns (C11, C13, C16, C18) are the outbound clip played backwards; each was inspected frame by frame:
the camera backs out along the same path, the door swings shut after the camera has cleared it, and the end frame is the exact start frame of the outbound clip.
C20 (plan return) was generated separately because it came out cleaner than a reversed C01.
"End match" is the structural similarity (SSIM, 1.0 = identical) between the last frame and the target still; every first frame matched its start still at 0.987 or better.
The attempt limit was three per clip for the same pair of images. No clip needed more; C12 was regenerated from scratch (three new attempts) when its end image was replaced. No clip is missing.
